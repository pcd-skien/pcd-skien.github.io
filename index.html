Cinema across borders. Stories beyond erasure. / Film over landegrenser. Historier som ikke kan utviskes.
I 2023 måtte den palestinske filmfestivalen Palestine Cinema Days gå i eksil – til hele verden. Den 2. november 2026 vil den foregå i 78 land samtidig, og som i Houston, Dhaka, Napoli og Dar es Salaam, får vi i Skien mulighet til å oppleve Palestinsk filmkunst.

Join us in Skien for this special screening on the 109th anniversary of the Balfour Declaration. We invite you to witness stories of resilience, memory, and hope.

With Hasan in Gaza

2025 | Documentary | 106 min | Dir. Kamal Aljafari
Watch Trailer ↗

Three MiniDV tapes of life in Gaza from 2001 were recently rediscovered. A search for a former prison mate from 1989, leads to an unexpected road trip from the north to the south of Gaza with Hasan, a local guide whose fate remains unknown.
