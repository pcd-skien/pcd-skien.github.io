About This Instance

Palestine Cinema Days Around the World is a global platform that transcends borders to bring Palestinian stories to the forefront of the world’s conscience.

Join us in Skien for this special screening on the 109th anniversary of the Balfour Declaration. We invite you to witness stories of resilience, memory, and hope.
